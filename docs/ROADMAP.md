# Financial-model roadmap

## 1. English language foundation

Continue the current SSM-JEPA English model. Improve grammatical consistency
and coherent generation on reserved prompts, alongside held-out language loss.
Keep training, recovery and recurrent generation reproducible.

## 2. Financial specialization

Prepare appropriately licensed English filings, annual reports, accounting
material and macroeconomic publications. Preserve document provenance,
reporting periods, publication dates, units and tables.

Develop document-grounded financial explanations and factual extraction.
Reserve documents and historical periods for evaluation.

## 3. Quantitative and fundamental analysis

Integrate structured numerical evidence explicitly. The current byte-level
language model is not a specialized price-series forecasting model.

Support returns, volatility, drawdowns, statement analysis, cash flows and
valuation. Verify arithmetic using reproducible calculations. Evaluate market
hypotheses chronologically, including transaction costs and leakage checks.

## 4. Macro context and portfolio risk

Connect rates, inflation, growth and liquidity scenarios to asset exposures.
Distinguish observed information from scenario assumptions. Examine
concentration, diversification and downside sensitivity.

## 5. Integrated research

Produce one analysis covering quantitative evidence, business fundamentals
and macro risk. Explain disagreements, missing information and sensitivities.
Every analysis must state its information cutoff date and sources.

Portfolio optimization and order execution require separate implementation
and evaluation; they are not current capabilities.

## 6. Controlled continual learning

Update the model from traceable new documents. Measure retained language
and financial abilities after updates. Preserve versioned checkpoints and
control temporal leakage. Generated answers are not automatically accepted
as training truth.

## Delivery principle

Keep each change connected to the financial language model: a usable
capability, a reliability improvement or evidence needed to assess it.
Language-loss improvements alone do not establish financial reasoning or
investment performance.
